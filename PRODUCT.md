# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AcornOps platform administrators handling bounded support, security, and governance requests. They work under time pressure and need to inspect platform-level identities, workspace access, plans, lifecycle state, and privileged audit history without entering tenant operations.

## Product Purpose

Provide a governance-only console for safe, auditable administration of existing users and workspaces without granting platform administrators access to workspace operational content. Success means an administrator can understand scope, make one intentional change, and verify its outcome while tenant data and operational controls remain unavailable. Platform administrators must not run commands that modify workloads.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The console should feel familiar to AcornOps operators, visibly privileged without being alarming, and direct about consequences.

## Anti-references

- Consumer-style account settings that hide scope or consequences.
- Security dashboards that use dramatic color, decoration, or fear as hierarchy.
- Generic admin templates that obscure AcornOps identity or mix governance with tenant operations.
- Dense mutation surfaces where destructive actions compete with routine inspection.

## Design Principles

- Keep the administrator in the object context where the task began.
- Make inspection the default and reveal mutation controls deliberately.
- State actor, subject, scope, and consequence in plain language.
- Preserve one shared interaction vocabulary across workspace-centric and user-centric access tasks.
- Continuously reinforce the governance boundary without interrupting the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus, explicit accessible names, screen-reader announcements for asynchronous state changes, non-color state cues, reduced motion, responsive reflow, and readable contrast in bright office conditions.
